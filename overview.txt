Tax Types – pre-filled with No Tax / 5% / 10% / 15% / 18%. You can add new tax types in the empty rows (enter the name and the rate as a decimal, e.g. 0.20 for 20%).
Partners – all the fields you listed:
Partner Name (required – yellow cells)
EDB Tax ID
EMBS Registry Number
Address, City, Country
Phone, Mobile, Email
(All other fields are optional so you can fill them later.)

Measurement Places – 8 places (PlaceID 1–8). Enter the place names.
Invoices – Invoice Number, Created Date, Due Date, PlaceID (dropdown 1–8), PartnerID. Place name and invoice totals fill automatically.
Invoice Lines – for the articles on each invoice:
InvoiceID (link to the invoice)
Article Type / Description
Quantity
Amount Without Tax
Tax Type Name (dropdown)
Tax Rate, Tax Amount and Amount With Tax are calculated automatically
(Amount With Tax = Amount Without Tax + Tax based on the selected rate)

Reports
All-time totals by measurement place + Grand Total
Date-filtered totals (set From Date and To Date at the top)
For yearly reports just set the dates to 01.01.YYYY – 31.12.YYYY


How to use it

Yellow cells = enter data
Green cells = calculated (do not edit)
Dates are in DD.MM.YYYY format
You can add more rows later by copying the formulas down
Tax dropdown currently has the default rates; if you add new tax types, type the exact name you entered in the Tax Types sheet

Everything is formula-driven, so totals and reports update automatically as you enter invoices and line items.
